- **Home Assistant guide: an Alexa route with no subscription or skill.** The
  Alexa section now describes the community Home-Assistant-Matter-Hub add-on,
  which publishes RuView's motion-class presence sensor to a Matter-capable
  Echo over the local network. The guide notes that the add-on needs Home
  Assistant OS (a virtual machine on Windows), IPv6 and mDNS, and that it is
  untested with RuView.
